## Findings and disposition

| Severity and scope | Finding | Disposition for v1 |
|---|---|---|
| Medium for future runs | The protocol invalidates a candidate if a required base fit fails, and aborts if an anchor fails or no candidate remains. The selector aborts after a calibration-score base-fit failure for the chosen structure. A failed final non-anchor refit also aborts. [Protocol](protocol.json#L675) · [Selector](../code/nudgewell_hw5/selector.py#L82) · [Final refit](../code/nudgewell_hw5/selector.py#L113) | Zero base-fit jobs failed in the completed run. Retain the v1 checkpoints. Before reusing this implementation on another run, bring the fallback into line with the protocol and test both failure paths. |
| Low for future runs | The structural selector compares each score to the current winner plus the tolerance. With a chain of near ties, this can differ from choosing the earliest candidate within tolerance of the true maximum. [Protocol](protocol.json#L686) · [Selector](../code/nudgewell_hw5/selector.py#L65) | All five winners are separated from the next candidate by more than the tolerance. Before reuse, compute the maximum before applying the tie rule. |
| Low for future runs | The protocol specifies a base probability clipping range. The base predictor returns raw probabilities, while the sigmoid objective and application clip before taking logits. [Protocol](protocol.json#L671) · [Base prediction](../code/nudgewell_hw5/evaluation_contract.py#L59) · [Sigmoid fit](../code/nudgewell_hw5/evaluation_contract.py#L226) | Every stored raw candidate score and all final procedures' raw training and outer predictions lie inside the declared range. Raw nested auxiliary scores were not checkpointed individually. The sigmoid objective and application apply the declared clipping to those inputs before their logits are used. Record the discrepancy for correction and boundary testing before reuse. |
| Low for future runs | Reliability edges generated with NumPy can differ by one floating-point step from the protocol's literal edges at 0.3, 0.6 and 0.7. [Metric code](../code/nudgewell_hw5/metrics.py#L62) · [Frozen bin edges](protocol.json#L832) | No saved predictions change bins under the literal edges. The bin moments reconcile. Before reuse, read the frozen edges directly and test exact decimal boundaries. |
| Low, run artifact | The selection runner writes a fold manifest with an empty day list. Step 6 writes private states and predictions and leaves that list empty. [Manifest write](../code/nudgewell_hw5/runner.py#L199) · [Run artifact requirement](protocol.json#L893) | Added an ignored day-manifest supplement inside the run. It matches the protocol, row keys and outer scopes of the Step 4 and selection manifests. All 423 histories and 3,772 test positions match the saved states and predictions. SHA-256 is `0d0870b47921cfdb22f0ecb6494d5ce849f7413f18595d16fe52343c3cb3a7ff`. Original run artifacts retain their recorded hashes. |

## Conclusions the evidence supports

- **New-user ranking.** Balanced AP was 0.1558 for the refined selector and 0.1727 for the fixed baseline. The −0.0169 difference has a paired interval of [−0.0652, +0.0222]. The experiment did not establish an AP gain. Lower Brier and log loss are descriptive results for the complete refined procedure. This comparison changes the model-selection procedure, feature pack and calibration together. [Aggregate evaluation](results/hw5-v1-step5-20261117_evaluation.json)
- **Daily probability accuracy.** The daily offset reduced balanced Brier loss by 0.00159 versus the smoothed personal base rate, with a paired interval of [0.00048, 0.00275]. Brier loss was lower for 20 people and higher for 17 in this cohort. The offset preserved within-day ranking relative to the global model, and the mean within-person AUC across days was lower than the global model's. The gain applies to the average probability error in this retrospective comparison. [Evaluation review](evaluation_review.md)
- **Operating comparison.** The fixed rule, fixed baseline and refined model used different observed alert shares. Their precision and recall describe those respective operating points. The daily comparison reuses the same global-model cutoff within each person's fold. The bootstrap holds fitted models fixed and reflects between-participant variation conditional on the saved predictions. The peer archive and retrospective design limit claims about prospective operation or the effect of intervening. [Evaluation review](evaluation_review.md)

The lead incorporated the participant-level variation and exact within-day tie check in the evaluation review. The run artifact gap is resolved by the private supplement. The dormant implementation discrepancies require no recalculation of the saved v1 results. The independent review fulfills Step 7 for this run. [Evaluation review](evaluation_review.md)
